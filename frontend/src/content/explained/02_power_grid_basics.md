# The Power Grid in Plain Words

A power grid moves electricity from power plants to homes and towns. Think of power like water flowing in pipes.

### Main Parts

- **Generators:** Power plants that make electricity.
- **Buses:** Junction points where wires meet.
- **Lines:** Long high-voltage wires that carry power.
- **Loads:** Towns and factories that use power.

### Pressure and Heartbeat

- **Voltage:** The electrical push in the wires. Safe levels stay near 1.0 per-unit.
- **Frequency:** The steady heartbeat of the grid. In North America, it runs at 60 Hz. When power made equals power used, frequency stays flat.

*Where the analogy ends:* Electricity flows along all closed paths at once.
