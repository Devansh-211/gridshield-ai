# The AI Analyst, Honestly

GridShield includes an AI helper that writes clear notes for people.

### What the AI Does
- It reads verified numbers from math checks.
- It writes clear notes in plain words.
- It cites exact clues like `[E1]` and `[E2]`.

### What the AI Does NOT Do
- It does not calculate power flow.
- It does not pick alarm levels.
- It cannot invent numbers. All facts are checked before you see them.
