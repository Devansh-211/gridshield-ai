# Alarms and Risk Scores

GridShield uses five alarm levels to keep users alert without causing fatigue.

### Five Alarm Levels
- **Critical (Red):** High danger of blackout or equipment breakdown.
- **High (Orange):** Big shift that needs prompt action.
- **Medium (Amber):** Reading near allowable safety limit.
- **Low (Cyan):** Small meter drift or minor warning.
- **Advisory (Gray):** Simple status note.

### The Risk Score
The risk score runs from 0 to 100 based on voltage errors, line loads, frequency shifts, and meter trust.
